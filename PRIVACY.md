# SyntaxMemo Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 November 2026**

SyntaxMemo is a Chrome extension that allows users to capture, save, organise, and retrieve programming syntax snippets.

## Information Storage

SyntaxMemo stores snippets and associated metadata locally in the user's browser using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API.

This information remains on the user's device. SyntaxMemo does not operate a server or transmit saved snippets to external services.

## Data Collection and Sharing

SyntaxMemo does not collect, sell, or share personal information with third parties.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or user accounts.

## Data Control

Users can view, edit, copy, and delete their saved snippets through the extension dashboard. Uninstalling the extension may remove its locally stored data.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the publisher through the Chrome Web Store support contact.
